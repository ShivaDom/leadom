How to Write a Freelance Proposal That Wins the Job

Clients skim. A winning proposal is short, specific, and about their problem, not your resume.

1. Start with their goal

Open with one sentence restating what they want to achieve. It shows you read the brief.

2. State deliverables clearly

List exactly what they receive: files, number of revisions, formats. Vague scope causes disputes later.

3. Give a timeline with dates

Use real dates, not "a few days". Concrete plans build trust.

4. Show one relevant example

One similar project with a result beats five unrelated ones.

5. Price with options

Offer two or three tiers. Many clients pick the middle one, and the choice makes them feel in control.

6. End with one next step

Ask for approval and a deposit with a single clear action.
