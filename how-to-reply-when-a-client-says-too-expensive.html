How to Reply When a Client Says "Too Expensive"

"Too expensive" rarely means "no". It usually means the client does not yet see the value, or has a fixed budget.

Option 1: Adjust the scope, not the price

Offer a lighter version that fits the budget. You keep your rate and the client gets a smaller deliverable.

Option 2: Split the payment

Keep the full scope and propose two or three instalments. Cash flow is often the real issue.

Option 3: Explain the result

Connect your price to an outcome the client cares about: time saved, sales gained, risk avoided.

Option 4: Walk away politely

If the budget is far below your minimum, decline kindly and leave the door open.

Never discount without removing something. Lowering the price for the same work teaches the client your first price was negotiable.
