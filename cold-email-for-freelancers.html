Cold Email for Freelancers: A Simple 4-Line Formula

Long cold emails get deleted. Use four lines.

Line 1: A real observation

Mention something specific about their business. Generic flattery is obvious.

Line 2: The problem you solve

One sentence about a common problem for businesses like theirs.

Line 3: Proof

One similar result in plain words. Only claim what is true.

Line 4: A small ask

"Open to a 10-minute call this week?" is easier to answer than a big request.

Follow up twice

Send a short reminder after three days and a final note after a week. Many replies come from follow-ups.
