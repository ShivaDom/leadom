Overdue Invoice Reminder: What to Say at Day 1, 7 and 14

Most late payments are forgetfulness, not refusal. Escalate calmly and in writing.

Day 1 after the due date

Friendly reminder: mention the invoice number, amount and original due date, and ask if they need anything from you.

Day 7

Firmer tone: say the invoice is a week overdue, give a specific pay-by date, and say further work pauses until it is settled.

Day 14

Final notice: state the amount, the deadline, and any late fee you agreed in advance. Keep it factual.

Prevent the problem

Take a deposit before starting, put payment terms in writing, and send invoices the day work is delivered.
