How to Say No to a Client Politely

Saying no protects your time, your quality and your other clients.

Scope creep

Say the idea is good but outside the agreement, and offer to add it for a stated price and time.

Unrealistic deadline

Offer a realistic date, or a smaller version by their date.

A project that is not a fit

Thank them, explain you are not the right person, and suggest an alternative if you can.

A difficult client

End the relationship in writing, deliver what was paid for, and refund any unused amount.
